# Community contributions

Thank you to the contributors who investigated TikTok changes and proposed improvements.

- [chatterchats, PR #11](https://github.com/gabireze/tiktok-all-reposted-videos-remover/pull/11): proposed Firefox compatibility and clearer popup organization. The Chrome MV3 package keeps its service worker manifest; Firefox support needs a separately validated build.
- [FapCod, PR #13](https://github.com/gabireze/tiktok-all-reposted-videos-remover/pull/13): proposed a redesigned interface, more filtering options, reporting, and Spanish localization. The extension retains its dedicated repost-removal purpose and the current page-by-page workflow.

These PRs are part of the repository's merge history. Their older implementations are not included wholesale because the extension changed substantially afterward.
